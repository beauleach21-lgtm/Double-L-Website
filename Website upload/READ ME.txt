DOUBLE L — THE WEBSITE
======================

Everything in this folder IS the website. Nothing else is needed.
Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (not the folder itself) to the web
root of the hosting account, so that index.html sits at the top level.

Then https://yourdomain.com/ serves index.html, and a wrong address
serves 404.html.

WHAT'S HERE
  index.html          the whole website — all the design and behaviour
  404.html            the wrong-address page
  favicon-*.png       the tab icon, three sizes
  og.png              the picture that appears when the link is shared
  assets/day/         the five machines and the crew shot
  assets/fonts/       the six type files the site uses
  assets/heroes/      the hero reel: one still and five clips
  app/config.js       one setting — the address the enquiry form posts to

DO NOT RENAME OR REARRANGE ANYTHING. The paths inside index.html point
at these exact names.

THE THREE FAVICON LINKS AND og:image ARE ABSOLUTE (/favicon-32.png).
They resolve correctly at a domain root. If the site is ever put in a
SUBFOLDER instead, those four paths need the subfolder adding.

BEFORE IT GOES LIVE
  · Do not touch the MX records on the old domain. Email runs on
    doublelequipmentrentco.com and a dead inbox is worse than a late
    website.
  · The enquiry form posts to the Google Sheet. Send one test enquiry
    from the live site and confirm the row arrives.
  · The hero clips are about 18 MB in total. They load only when the
    reel reaches them, but on a slow phone connection the reel will be
    late. Compressing them is the single biggest speed win available.

TO REBUILD THIS FOLDER after changing the site:
  python3 "Double L Site/make-website-folder.py"
It is rebuilt from scratch each time, so anything added here by hand
is lost. Make changes in "Double L Site", never here.
